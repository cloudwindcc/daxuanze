How should you make personal finance and investment choices? For personal finance choices, look first at your risk tolerance and cash flow, and only then at returns. Long-term asset allocation matters more than short-term forecasts, and any investment should take into account the worst case, liquidity and the cost of your own time and energy. Should you take part in an investment opportunity if the risk is too high? For a high-risk investment opportunity, first check whether the worst-case outcome is bearable, whether you understand the underlying asset, whether the exit mechanism is clear, and whether it would affect your family's reserve funds. An opportunity you cannot understand, cannot exit, and cannot afford to lose should not be taken with a large position, even if the returns look high. Before High-Return Financial Products, Ask About the Worst Outcome The first principle of financial decisions is not maximising returns, but living long enough and keeping cash flow intact.
