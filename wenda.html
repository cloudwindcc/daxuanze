An opportunity you cannot understand, cannot exit, and cannot afford to lose should not be taken with a large position, even if the returns look high.

Q66

What should you do when there are too many options and you do not know how to choose?

When there are too many options, first use your bottom line to filter out unqualified options, then use the weighting of your goals to keep 2 to 3 candidates. Do not compare all options evenly; first determine the variable you really want to optimize: income, freedom, growth, stability, relationships or health.
