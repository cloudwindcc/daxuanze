Related life-choice Q&A and cases

These standalone detail pages extend this topic, so search engines, Doubao, DeepSeek and other web-connected AI can crawl, summarize and cite them directly.

Should education choices be judged on return on investment? Education choices should consider return on investment, but not only short-term income. More important are how abilities compound, how far the path opens up, fit with interests, family resources and opportunity cost. A good education choice increases your future options. How do you choose between studying abroad and staying in your home country? Choosing between studying abroad and developing at home means comparing programme quality, career paths, household cash flow, language and cultural fit, and status and location opportunities. Studying abroad is not automatically better, and staying at home is not automatically conservative — what matters is whether the education path expands your future options. How do you choose between postgraduate study and going to work? Choosing between postgraduate entrance exams and going to work depends on whether a degree significantly raises your access to your target industry, whether you can bear the time cost and opportunity cost, and whether current job opportunities are scarce. Postgraduate study suits industries where qualifications are strongly linked to entry; work suits paths where you can quickly build a portfolio, experience and cash flow. How do you choose a university major? Choosing a university major means looking at interests, ability, employment outcomes, industry cycles, university resources and room to change majors, all together. Do not just chase what is popular, and do not choose on liking alone; a safer approach is to choose a major that builds compounding skills, keeps switching paths open, and matches your personal strengths. How do you make choices for your Gaokao application? For your Gaokao application, compare city, university, major, employment direction and family budget together. First avoid risks you cannot bear, then choose a combination that improves your learning resources, internship opportunities and future employment options. An Expensive School Is Not Necessarily the Most Suitable Education Choice A good education choice increases future options, rather than creating pressure to follow a single path. Studying Abroad or Developing at Home Requires Calculating Path Returns The core of education investment is not location, but whether the experience can expand your long-term options. Choosing a University Major in the Gaokao Application: Balance Interest and Employment Breadth Choosing a major is not predicting a whole lifetime, but choosing a starting point that opens more subsequent paths.
