Should you leave your current company?

Direct answer

To judge whether to leave a company, see whether your current role is still increasing your ability, income, resources and future options. If it is only short-term discomfort, first try improving communication and responsibilities; if it is a long-term drain, growth has stalled and outside opportunities are better, prepare a departure plan with a buffer.
