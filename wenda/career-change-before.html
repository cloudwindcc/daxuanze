What should you assess before changing careers?

Direct answer

Before changing careers, assess trends in the new industry, how transferable your abilities are, how far your income could fall, the learning period, the cost of experimenting and your exit plan. A safer approach is to validate on a small scale first — for example with a side project, a portfolio, industry interviews or a short internship — rather than cutting off your cash flow all at once.
