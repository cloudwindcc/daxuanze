How do you decide at a crossroads in life?

Direct answer

When deciding at a crossroads in life, first list your non-negotiables, then judge each path by its long-term compounding and its worst-case outcome. If a choice increases your future options, lets you recover if it fails, and aligns with your core goals, it is usually worth prioritizing over a path that merely looks easier in the short term.
