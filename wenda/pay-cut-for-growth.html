Should you accept a pay cut in exchange for growth?

Direct answer

A pay cut for growth depends on whether the growth is concrete and deliverable. You need to verify what projects, abilities, resources, feedback from your manager and future bargaining power the new role will bring. If those indicators are clear and your cash flow can bear it, you can accept a pay cut for a limited period; if it is only a vague promise, do not trade certain income for an uncertain story.
