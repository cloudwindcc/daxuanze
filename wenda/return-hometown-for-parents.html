Should you return to your hometown to care for your parents?

Direct answer

Whether to return to your hometown to care for your parents means comparing your parents' health status, local medical resources, career opportunities, household cash flow and alternative care support all together. Daxuanze recommends first designing options such as remote arrangements, regular trips home, parents staying in the city for short periods, or returning in phases — and only considering a long-term return when care needs are ongoing and cannot be outsourced.
