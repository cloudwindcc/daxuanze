What should you do when your career development stalls?

Direct answer

When career development stalls, first judge whether it is caused by the boundaries of your role, your manager's resources, the industry cycle or the structure of your own abilities. You can first seek new projects, fill key skill gaps, expand your sample of external interviews and industry information, and then decide on an internal move, a job change or a transition.
