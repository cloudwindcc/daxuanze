What should you do if your partner is unsure about getting married?

Direct answer

When a partner is unsure about marriage, the point is not to force an answer, but to break the uncertainty down: is it a difference in values, financial pressure, family boundaries, trust issues, or a different sense of timing for the future? Problems that can be discussed and repaired can be tested further; problems that are avoided over the long term call for caution.
