Should you end a draining relationship?

Direct answer

To judge whether to end a draining relationship, see whether it has long drained your emotions, time and opportunity cost, and whether boundaries cannot be formed through communication. If the other person persistently denies the problem, crosses boundaries or keeps taking, reducing your investment protects your long-term state better than maintaining the relationship on the surface.
