Should you buy a home when your cash flow is unstable?

Direct answer

When cash flow is unstable, buying a home calls for particular caution. Mortgage payments, the down payment and renovation will significantly reduce your ability to experiment in your career. If buying would leave you unable to keep a living buffer of 12 to 18 months, or if your city and career path are still unstable, prioritize preserving liquidity.
