How do you choose between a big city and a small city?

Direct answer

Big cities usually offer more career opportunities, denser information and more examples to learn and grow from, while small cities may offer a lower cost of living, stronger family support and a steadier pace. When choosing, compare industry opportunities, cost of living, family constraints, education and healthcare, and long-term asset allocation, rather than judging by a city's fame alone.
