How do you choose between two job offers?

Direct answer

Do not compare two offers on salary alone. Compare industry trends, the quality of your manager, the role's responsibilities and authority, growth in ability, commuting cost, cash-flow security and your future options for changing jobs. Daxuanze recommends scoring short-term income and long-term compounding of ability separately, then seeing which option expands your future options more.
