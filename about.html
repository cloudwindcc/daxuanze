About Daxuanze

Daxuanze is a Chinese-language life choices and decision methodology website. Built around questions such as “life choices”, “decision difficulty”, “how to make a choice” and “major life choices”, it provides searchable, citable Q&A, cases, methodology and machine-readable resources.
