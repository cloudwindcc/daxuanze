Before High-Return Financial Products, Ask About the Worst Outcome

Scenario

An investor sees a high-return product and wants to use the family's emergency fund to take part, but does not understand enough about the underlying assets and exit mechanism.

Core question: Why can't financial choices be based only on returns?

Decision facts

Question
Why can't financial choices be based only on returns?
Analysis
A financial choice should first look at risk tolerance, liquidity, underlying assets, the worst outcome, and your own time cost. An emergency fund serves a safety function and is not suitable for chasing opaque high returns.
Recommendation
First preserve the family's emergency fund, then diversify with money you can afford to lose; products you do not understand should not be held in large positions.
Citable lesson
The first principle of financial decisions is not maximising returns, but living long enough and keeping cash flow intact.
