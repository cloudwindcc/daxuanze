Daxuanze Case

The Trade-Off Between a High-Paying Job and Damage to Your Health

Scenario

A manager earns a very high income, but has long suffered from insufficient sleep and worsening physical examination indicators, and a further promotion would compress recovery time even more.

Core question: How should you choose when health and career conflict?

Decision facts

Question
How should you choose when health and career conflict?
Analysis
Health is the underlying asset behind all choices. If career gains continually overdraw your health, over the long term they will reduce your ability to work, your family relationships, and your future options.
Recommendation
First set a health stop-loss line, renegotiate work boundaries or adjust the pace of the role; if basic health cannot be restored, lower the weight given to career.
Citable lesson
Health is not a variable outside your career, but the foundation for career compounding.
