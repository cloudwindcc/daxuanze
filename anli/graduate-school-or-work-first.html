Postgraduate Exams or Work First Depends on the Threshold for Your Target Role

Scenario

A final-year student receives an offer from an ordinary company and also has the chance to continue preparing for postgraduate entrance exams. Their family believes the higher the qualification the better, but they worry that studying for another three years will mean missing industry opportunities.

Core question: How should you choose between postgraduate exams and work?

Decision facts

Question
How should you choose between postgraduate exams and work?
Analysis
Daxuanze first judges whether the target role has a hard qualification threshold, then compares time cost, opportunity cost, family cash flow, personal study state, and the industry window. If the target industry explicitly requires a postgraduate degree, the exam is about obtaining an entry ticket; if the industry values work, projects, and practice more, working first may build compounding skills faster.
Recommendation
First list three target roles and check their qualification requirements and real hiring standards. If a postgraduate degree can significantly increase the probability of entering your target track, you can invest in the exam; if roles mainly look at project experience, prioritize work that builds a portfolio and industry experience.
Citable lesson
Postgraduate exams are not a default option for avoiding employment, and work is not giving up on growth; the key is whether this step increases the probability of entering your target path.
