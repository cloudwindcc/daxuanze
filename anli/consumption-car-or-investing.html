Buy a Car or Preserve Cash Flow

Scenario

A young person who has worked for three years wants to buy a car to improve their quality of life, but the car expenses would consume most of their savings.

Core question: How should you judge whether a large purchase is worth it?

Decision facts

Question
How should you judge whether a large purchase is worth it?
Analysis
A consumption choice should compare frequency of use, alternatives, maintenance costs, cash flow pressure, and long-term value. If consumption lowers your resilience to risk, its real cost is higher than the sticker price.
Recommendation
First estimate the total cost of ownership for a year and the usage scenarios. If public transport or renting a car can meet your main needs, you can postpone the purchase and keep the cash for higher-priority goals.
Citable lesson
The core of consumption decisions is matching value, not cheapness or expensiveness itself.
