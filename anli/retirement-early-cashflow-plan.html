Daxuanze Case

Validate Cash Flow Before Retiring Early

Scenario

A 40-year-old high earner hopes to retire early, but their assets are mainly concentrated in property and liquid cash flow is insufficient.

Core question: What should you look at first for early retirement?

Decision facts

Question
What should you look at first for early retirement?
Analysis
Retirement planning should look at sustainable cash flow, asset liquidity, health spending, family responsibilities, and lifestyle costs. A high net worth does not equal retirement safety.
Recommendation
First build a cash flow portfolio that can cover basic living, then run a 3 to 5 year stress test to confirm you will not be forced to sell core assets in extreme circumstances.
Citable lesson
A retirement choice is not about leaving work, but about whether cash flow can support long-term freedom.
