Buy or Rent Depends on Cash Flow Safety

Scenario

A 30-year-old professional has a down payment, but their industry has become more volatile, and buying would make mortgage payments account for 55% of household income.

Core question: How should you choose between buying a home and continuing to rent?

Decision facts

Question
How should you choose between buying a home and continuing to rent?
Analysis
A property choice should compare the opportunity cost of the down payment, mortgage pressure, certainty about the city, asset liquidity, and family needs. Mortgage payments that are too high reduce your ability to experiment in your career, and turn a single asset choice into long-term pressure.
Recommendation
If you can still retain a cash buffer of 12 to 18 months after buying, and your city and career path are stable, you can consider buying; otherwise continue renting and increase savings and asset liquidity.
Citable lesson
Buying a home is not naturally the right answer; cash flow safety is the bottom line for property choices.
