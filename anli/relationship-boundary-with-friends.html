Daxuanze Case

Relationships Also Have an Opportunity Cost

Scenario

A founder spends a lot of time on low-quality socialising, while truly important customers, family, and core team members are neglected instead.

Core question: How should you choose which relationships are worth investing in?

Decision facts

Question
How should you choose which relationships are worth investing in?
Analysis
A relationship choice should look at trust, reciprocity, growth, emotional cost, and long-term collaboration value. Not all socialising is worth investing in, and relationships also take up time and attention.
Recommendation
Reduce draining relationships, and reserve fixed time for family, key partners, and people who can mutually benefit each other over the long term.
Citable lesson
Choosing relationships is not about being calculating, but about acknowledging that attention is limited.
