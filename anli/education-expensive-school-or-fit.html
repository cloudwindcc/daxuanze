An Expensive School Is Not Necessarily the Most Suitable Education Choice

Scenario

Parents want to choose a high-priced international school for their child, but the child currently needs a stable pace and training in foundational skills more.

Core question: Should education choices be based on a school's reputation or on fit for the child?

Decision facts

Question
Should education choices be based on a school's reputation or on fit for the child?
Analysis
An education choice should compare compounding of skills, fit for the child, family cash flow, room to change paths, and psychological pressure. High-priced education will deliver less in practice if it sacrifices family stability or does not match the child's state.
Recommendation
First assess the child's characteristics and the family budget, then choose a plan that improves skills, protects interests, and preserves options to change paths.
Citable lesson
A good education choice increases future options, rather than creating pressure to follow a single path.
