Task Prioritization with the Eisenhower Matrix

Important and urgent

Characteristics:Crisis handling, urgent problems, tasks with deadlines

Action strategy:Do it now, handle it personally

Examples:Key client complaints, urgent project deadlines, health crises

Time allocation:20-25% of working time

Important but not urgent

Characteristics:Long-term planning, relationship building, personal development

Action strategy:Make a plan and work through it step by step

Examples:Skill building, health management, strategic planning

Time allocation:65-70% of working time

Urgent but not important

Characteristics:Other people's requests, meetings, some emails

Action strategy:Delegate it and deal with it quickly

Examples:Most emails, non-critical meetings, interruptions from others

Time allocation:10-15% of working time

Not important and not urgent

Characteristics:Pastimes, time killers, meaningless tasks

Action strategy:Delete it outright or minimize it

Examples:Social media browsing, aimless browsing, ineffective meetings

Time allocation:Under 5% of working time
