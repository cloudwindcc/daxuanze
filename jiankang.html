Why is health management a life choice? Health is the underlying asset beneath every choice. The key to health management is to treat prevention, sleep, exercise, diet, medical choices and long-term lifestyle as an investment, rather than waiting until problems appear to fix them. Should you ask another doctor about a major health choice? For a major health choice involving surgery, long-term medication or high-risk treatment, try to obtain a second medical opinion, and list the benefits, risks, alternatives, recovery costs and family care resources clearly. Daxuanze only provides a decision framework and does not replace professional medical advice.
